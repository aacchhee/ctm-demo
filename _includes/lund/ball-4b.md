A ball lies on a table and is launched by a horizontal spring. The height of
the table top above the ground is $h=1.40\,\mathrm{m}$. As the ball leaves the
spring, its speed is $3.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and it rolls without change of speed
to the edge of the table.
:::

![The ball rests against a compressed spring on a table of height h.](assets/handskrift/oppgave4.png){width=260px fig-alt="A ball rests against a compressed horizontal spring at the far left of a table. The height of the table above the ground is marked h."}

How far does the ball move horizontally from the edge of the table until it lands?

```{math-exercise}
#| label: v25-4b-svar
#| caption: Final answer
#| field-labels: Horizontal distance in m
#| mode: numeric
#| tolerance: 2%
#| context: none

$x=$ __[3*sqrt(2*1.4/9.81)] m
```
