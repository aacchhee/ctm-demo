A ball is thrown straight up from a height of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the
ground. The initial speed of the ball is $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::

### a) The highest point

How long does it take until the ball is at its highest point? How high above
the ground is it then?

```{math-exercise}
#| label: v25-1a-svar
#| caption: a · Final answer
#| field-labels: Time in s, Height above the ground in m
#| mode: numeric
#| tolerance: 2%
#| context: none

$t=$ __[6.0/9.81] s, $h=$ __[1.5+36/(2*9.81)] m
```
