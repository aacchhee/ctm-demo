### a) The resistance R₂

::: {#v25-oppg6a .ai-context}
Two resistors are connected in series to a voltage source with a terminal
voltage of $230\,\mathrm{V}$. The voltage across $R_1$ is
$U_1=150\,\mathrm{V}$, and the current is $10\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::

How large is the resistance $R_2$?

```{math-exercise}
#| label: v25-6a-svar
#| caption: a · Final answer
#| field-labels: Resistance R2 in ohm
#| mode: numeric
#| tolerance: 2%
#| context: none

$R_2=$ __[(230-150)/10] Ω
```
