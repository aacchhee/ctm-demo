Solve the problems from the practice exam on paper. The problems are below;
the [original exam (PDF, Norwegian)](../assets/handskrift/proveeksamen-v2025.pdf)
is available too. For each subtask you can

1. write the final answer in the answer field and choose **Check** (use a
   decimal point, and do not write the unit in the field);
2. take a photo of your working, upload it under **Upload images** and choose
   **Feedback**. Each new click gives a deeper hint: first an assessment, then
   an explanation, then the method and finally a worked solution.
   The **Feedback language** menu next to each upload field chooses the
   language the AI answers in (English, Norwegian, Swedish, Spanish, Russian
   or German) for that subtask only.

If the AI misreads or misunderstands your uploaded work, choose **The AI
misunderstood my work** below its feedback. Point to the line or detail it
missed. You get one correction reply, and the hints restart at level 1.
To have changed or additional work assessed, upload a new page. This correction
feature is available with direct API feedback.

The feedback comes from an AI model you choose yourself under the gear ⚙
(direct API with your own key, or “copy prompt” to an AI chat). The model must
be able to read images. The image and the problem text are sent to the provider
you choose. The AI can be wrong: read the feedback critically.

::: {.callout-note collapse="true"}
## For teachers: test with real answers

The buttons **Fill in student 1** and **Fill in student 2** load real,
anonymised answers from two students to problems 1–4 (handwritten in
Norwegian), with the photo and the final values, so you do not have to upload
anything. The buttons at the top fill in every subtask at once. The teacher's
assessment of each answer is in the
[benchmark sheet](https://github.com/aacchhee/ctm-demo/blob/main/docs/handwriting-benchmark.md).
The prompts are YAML files in the repository; see
[Playing with the prompts](handwriting-ai-prompts.qmd). The prompt sandbox at the
bottom of the page lets you try changes without rebuilding the site.
:::
