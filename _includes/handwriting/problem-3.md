The mass of an average nitrogen molecule ($\mathrm{N_2}$) is
$28.0\,\mathrm{u}$. An amount of $4.50\cdot10^{23}$ molecules of nitrogen gas
is enclosed in a cylinder with radius $12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height
$20.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. The cylinder is kept in a room at normal air pressure
($101\,\mathrm{kPa}$), and the pressure of the gas is $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::

### a) Temperature and density

Calculate the temperature and the density of the gas.

```{math-exercise}
#| label: v25-3a-svar
#| caption: a · Final answer
#| field-labels: Temperature in K, Density in kg/m³
#| mode: numeric
#| tolerance: 2%
#| context: none

$T=$ __[120e3*pi*0.12**2*0.2/(4.5e23*1.380649e-23)] K,
$\rho=$ __[4.5e23*28*1.66054e-27/(pi*0.12**2*0.2)] kg/m³
```

### b) Mass of the piston

The lid of the cylinder is a piston that can slide up and down without
friction. What is the mass of the piston?

```{math-exercise}
#| label: v25-3b-svar
#| caption: b · Final answer
#| field-labels: Mass of the piston in kg
#| mode: numeric
#| tolerance: 2%
#| context: none

$m=$ __[(120e3-101e3)*pi*0.12**2/9.81] kg
```
