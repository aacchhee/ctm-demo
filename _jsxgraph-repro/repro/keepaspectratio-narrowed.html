keepaspectratio board narrowed below the box's aspect ratio: sides cropped

JSXGraph version: . Other versions: 1.12.2 · 1.13.3 · 1.14.0 · 1.14.1 · 1.14.2

Press Narrow A and compare (A) with (B).

(A) Created 600 px wide, then narrowed

(B) Created 273 px wide (reference)

The same board twice: boundingbox: [0, 6, 14, 0] (14 wide, 6 high), keepaspectratio: true, in a div 150 px high, with axes and a grid. The red lines and labels mark the box's left edge (x = 0) and right edge (x = 14); the blue curve runs from one to the other. (B) is created at 273 px: initBoard fits the whole box (x 0…14). (A) is created at 600 px, where the box is widened to x −5…19, and is then narrowed to 273 px by its container, as on a phone or a narrower window. Nothing else happens; JSXGraph's ResizeObserver does the resize.

Expected: after narrowing, (A) shows both red edges, both labels and the whole curve, as (B) does.
Seen (1.12.2 to 1.14.2, Chromium): (A) shows x 1.54…12.46 and y 0…6: both red edges, both labels and the curve's ends are cut off (all five versions linked above). With 1.14.1 and 1.14.2, widening (A) again restores the earlier view, and a board zoomed in first is cropped the same way.

A board created at the narrow width, such as (B), is not cropped: the problem appears only when a board is narrowed after it was created (a narrower window, a phone rotated from landscape to portrait).

Cause (v1.14.2): resizeContainer re-applies the current box with setBoundingBox(box, keepaspectratio, 'keep') (board.js 5863–5864). For a container wider than high, setBoundingBox keeps the vertical extent (unitY = h / dy) whatever the box's horizontal extent then needs (board.js 6550–6560), so the rule follows the container's orientation, not whether the box still fits. The "switch dominating interval" branch only applies when the box itself is higher than wide. initBoard instead fits the whole box with one scale (min of w/dx and h/dy).

Possible fix: on resize, fit the whole intended box with one scale, as initBoard does; this needs the board to remember the intended box (the one its code, initBoard or the user's zoom and pan set).
