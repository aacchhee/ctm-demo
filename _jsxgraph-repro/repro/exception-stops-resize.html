One exception during a resize stops all later resizing

JSXGraph version: . Other versions: 1.9.1 · 1.13.3 · 1.14.0 · 1.14.0 without the error (reference) · overview

A visible board with a text whose content function throws once, during the second of four automatic container resizes (600 → 500 → 400 → 700 → 300 px, one every 400 ms). Nothing to click.

Expected: after the failed update, the board follows the next resizes again.
Seen: after the resize in which the error happened, the SVG keeps its size; the status marks every later step "not following". Without the error (reference link) every step follows. The only trace is a console.log of the error (1.11.0 and later).
