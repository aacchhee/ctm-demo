Board in a sandboxed iframe in a hidden tab: blank when the tab is opened

JSXGraph version: . Other versions: 1.9.1 · 1.9.2 · 1.13.3 · 1.14.0 · overview

Tab 1: no board here. Click Tab 2 (board).

Status: Tab 2 not opened yet.

A tab pane is display:none when the page loads. It holds a sandboxed data: iframe (500×300 px) whose page loads JSXGraph and creates a board in a div of 100%×100%, as the Quarto jsxgraph extension embeds boards.

  1. Open this page in desktop Chrome and keep the area above in view.
  2. Click Tab 2 (board). A second later the status line says what the iframe shows.
  3. Or press Run 20 times automatically: each trial puts a new hidden pane with the same iframe at the top of the page, shows it 1.5 s later, and reads the board 2 s after that.

Expected: axes and a sine curve.
Seen with 1.9.2 to 1.14.0: when the iframe is in view at the moment its pane is shown, the frame stays white: the status says BLANK (the board's div has its size, its SVG is 0×0). In desktop Chrome 152 on Windows the Run 20 times automatically button gave: JSXGraph 1.14.0 blank in 20 of 20, JSXGraph 1.9.1 blank in 0 of 20; a manual click on Tab 2 was blank as well. Chromium 141 on Linux under automation shows it less reliably: 40 to 63 % of page loads in an independent verification (30 loads per cell), 2 of 20 with the button. Clicking Tab 1 and Tab 2 again does not bring the board back; a change of the iframe's size does. With 1.9.1 the board is drawn, though showing the shifted region of the 1.6.0–1.9.1 item listed under "Fixed in later JSXGraph versions" on the overview page.

Why the position matters. Chrome does not render a cross-origin iframe that is outside the visible area. If the pane is opened while the iframe is below the visible area, the iframe's window stays 0×0 until it is scrolled into view, and JSXGraph then receives one clean size and draws correctly. The status says "not yet laid out" in that case. This is why the problem can look intermittent on longer pages.
