Board in a sandboxed iframe in a hidden tab: blank when the tab is opened

JSXGraph version: . Other versions: 1.9.1 · 1.9.2 · 1.13.3 · 1.14.0 · overview

Tab 1: no board here. Click Tab 2 (board).

Status: Tab 2 not opened yet.

A tab pane is display:none when the page loads. It holds a sandboxed data: iframe (500×300 px) whose page loads JSXGraph and creates a board in a div of 100%×100%, as the Quarto jsxgraph extension embeds boards.

The board stays blank only if Chrome is not painting the window at the moment the pane is shown: the window is covered by another window or in the background, or the browser is headless or automated. Chrome still reports document.hidden = false then. In a window in front, the board is drawn. For a reliable reproduction in a visible window, see blank-after-quick-show.html: the same defect without an iframe.

  1. Open this page in desktop Chrome and press Run 20 times automatically. Each trial puts a new hidden pane with the same iframe at the top of the page, shows it 1.5 s later, and reads the board 2 s after that.
  2. While it runs, cover the browser window with another window for some of the trials. The result is "Blank in N of 20", with one line per trial.
  3. Or click Tab 2 (board); a second later the status line says what the iframe shows.

Expected: axes and a sine curve in every trial.
Seen with 1.9.2 to 1.14.0: the trials during which the window was covered stay white: the status says BLANK (the board's div has its size, its SVG is 0×0). In Chrome 152 on Windows with JSXGraph 1.14.0, checked by hand: blank in 4 of 20, exactly the trials while the window was covered; every board shown while the window was in front was drawn. In Chromium 141 on Linux under automation: blank in 40 to 63 % of page loads (an independent verification, 30 loads per cell). Clicking Tab 1 and Tab 2 again does not bring a blank board back; a change of the iframe's size does. With 1.9.1 the board is drawn, though showing the shifted region of the 1.6.0–1.9.1 item listed under "Fixed in later JSXGraph versions" on the overview page.

"Not yet laid out" in the status means that the iframe was still 0×0 when it was read, for example because it was outside the visible area.
