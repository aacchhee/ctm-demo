JSXGraph @VERSION@: board visible, hidden, hidden in an iframe

Plain HTML, no Quarto and no Quarto extension. JSXGraph @VERSION@ is loaded from jsDelivr with this exact version. Two boards, each placed three ways; all six are created at page load:

  • Solenoid (keepaspectratio: false), 100% wide (at most 760 px), 320 px high. Expected: 30 turns spread over the whole length, filling the frame, at every width.
  • Pendulum (keepaspectratio: true), 100% wide (at most 720 px), 390 px high. Expected: the cart fills most of the frame's height, as in (a), at every width.

To test: open the page in a wide window; press both Show buttons; then make the window narrow (phone width, about 390 px, or the device toolbar of the developer tools); then wide again. Compare (b) and (c) with (a).
