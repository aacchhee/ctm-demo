The board is defined by unitX: 50, unitY: 50, originX: 250, originY: 150 (no bounding box) and created in a div of width: 100%; height: 300px whose parent is display:none, so the div is 0 px wide at creation. A point P is at (1, 1).

  1. Press Show. The page reads the board 300 ms later.
  2. Then it narrows the container to 90% and reads the board again.

Expected: units 50 and 50, origin at (250, 150), so P is at screen position (300, 100), after showing and after the resize.
Seen: after showing, unitX is Infinity and P's position is NaN; after the next resize the board uses the default bounding box [−5, 5, 5, −5] instead of the given units and origin.
